SCAD Luxury & Brand Management · New York

New York,
Through Luxury

An introduction to the standards, stories, and people shaping contemporary luxury.

I couldn’t have asked for a better way to see New York for the first time.

As one of the selected candidates from SCAD’s Luxury and Brand Management program, I spent two days moving through a side of the city I had been curious about for years, stepping inside the worlds of Assouline, Bvlgari, Tiffany & Co., F. Schumacher, Printemps, and L’Oréal.

What stayed with me most was not just what each company does, but the standards behind how they do it. Through conversations with industry professionals, I gained a closer look at the values, expectations, and level of thought required to work in luxury: attention to detail, curiosity, precision, presence, and a genuine understanding of the customer and the story behind what you represent.

Seeing these qualities come through differently across publishing, jewelry, interiors, retail, and beauty made the trip feel less like a series of company visits and more like an introduction to the kind of professional I want to become.
